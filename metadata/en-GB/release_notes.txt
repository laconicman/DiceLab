First public build of DiceLab — a dice-physics sandbox that throws real 3D dice on a glass table.

• Two physics engines you can switch live: SceneKit and RealityKit
• Pace × viscosity sliders to tune how a throw feels
• Adjustable table size and dice count
• Everything runs on-device — no accounts, no network

Tell us how the throws feel — feedback shapes where the lab goes next.
